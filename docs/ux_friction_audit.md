### 📱 Usability & Friction Field Sheet: [TEST #01 - San Pablo Tijaltepec]

#### 1. Participant Profile
- **Community:** San Pablo Tijaltepec, Oaxaca
- **Producer / Artisan:** Doña Francisca (64 years old, traditional weaver & embroiderer)
- **Device used:** Android phone (6.5" screen) in natural daylight / open workshop
- **Previous tech experience:** Basic keypad phone for voice calls; minimal touch screen experience; uses voice notes in WhatsApp with help of relatives.

#### 2. Live Task Usability & Friction Results

| Task Evaluated | Time to Complete | Did they need help? | Observed Friction & Frustration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Task 1: Voice Note**<br>(Microphone recording) | **18 seconds** | Hesitated | Looked at the screen tentatively for over 10 seconds. Feared touching the button asking: *"¿Se va a desconfigurar el teléfono si le pico?"* (Will the phone break if I tap it?). Once reassured, spoke naturally in Mixteco (*Tu'un Savi*) explaining the animal figures on her blouse. |
| **Task 2: Digital Passport**<br>(Screen inspection) | **52 seconds** | Confused | Struggled to read the small typography under daylight reflection. Confused the official TecNM seal with a government tax inspection badge: *"¿Esto es para pagar impuestos en Hacienda?"*. Once clarified as authenticity protection against machine knockoffs, her perception shifted positively. |
| **Task 3: Physical QR Tag**<br>(Cardboard sample) | **6 seconds** | Understood immediately | Held the tag and instantly associated it with an authenticity seal: *"Si le colgamos esto a la blusa de venados, la gente ya no la confundirá con la ropa china que venden barata en el mercado"*. |

#### 3. The 3 Biggest Friction Points Detected
1. **Technophobia / Fear of error:** Reluctance to touch interactive elements out of fear of breaking the phone or deleting existing personal media.
2. **Contrast & Outdoor Glare:** High outdoor brightness in patios/workshops makes light gray subtexts and thin borders completely illegible for elderly eyes.
3. **Institutional Skepticism (Seals/Badges):** Generic or institutional badges cause apprehension (associated with fiscal audit or taxes) unless clearly explained as a cultural protection mark (*Sello de Autenticidad Comunitaria*).

#### 4. Proposed Immediate UX Fix
- **Microphone affordance:** Make the voice record button larger, with high-contrast icon and gentle pulsing animation to indicate it is safe to tap.
- **Visual accessibility:** Boost font sizes to minimum 16px/18px with high contrast ratios (dark forest greens / black against clean backgrounds) and avoid delicate thin fonts.
- **Clarity in labeling:** Replace institutional or technical labels with intuitive iconography and straightforward phrases like *"Bordado Auténtico Hecho a Mano"* and *"Sello de la Comunidad"*.

### 📱 Usability & Friction Field Sheet: [TEST #02 - San Juan Mixtepec]

#### 1. Participant Profile
- **Community:** San Juan Mixtepec, Mixteca Alta, Oaxaca
- **Producer / Artisan:** Doña Juana (68 years old, palm weaver / sombreros y tenates)
- **Device used:** Android phone (6.5" screen) under outdoor sunlight / earthen workshop patio
- **Previous tech experience:** Analog keypad phone for family calls; near zero touch experience; uses WhatsApp voice notes with grandchildren's help.

#### 2. Live Task Usability & Friction Results

| Task Evaluated | Time to Complete | Did they need help? | Observed Friction & Frustration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Task 1: Voice Note**<br>(Microphone recording) | **16 seconds** | Hesitated | Looked at the screen tentatively for 9 seconds. Asked: *"¿Si le aprieto aquí no se me borra nada del teléfono?"*. Once reassured, recorded naturally in Tu'un Savi (Mixteco) explaining the palm curing process. |
| **Task 2: Digital Passport**<br>(Screen inspection) | **48 seconds** | Confused | Sun glare made thin fonts hard to read. Feared the official TecNM seal meant tax inspections (*"¿Esto no es para que el gobierno me quite dinero?"*). Once explained it proves authenticity against plastic knockoffs, she welcomed it. |
| **Task 3: Physical QR Tag**<br>(Cardboard sample) | **5 seconds** | Understood immediately | Zero friction. Said: *"Si le amarramos este cartoncito al sombrero con un mecatito, quien lo compre en la ciudad sabrá que yo lo tejí en Mixtepec y no en una fábrica"*. |

#### 3. The 3 Biggest Friction Points Detected
1. **Fear of touching unknown buttons:** Anxiety about breaking device configuration or erasing files.
2. **Sunlight glare & contrast in field:** Gray subtexts and subtle borders disappear under midday sun in mountain patios.
3. **Institutional badge apprehension:** Unclear seals trigger concern about government taxes rather than community ownership.

#### 4. Proposed Immediate UX Fix
- **Prominent pulsing microphone:** Expand touch target to 64x64px with emerald green contrast and gentle pulsing ring.
- **High-contrast outdoor theme:** Dark green (`#032517`) and solid black text against crisp light backgrounds; minimum 16px font size.
- **Community-first terminology:** Emphasize *"Sello Comunitario"* and *"Voz de la Creadora"* over abstract technical metrics.
